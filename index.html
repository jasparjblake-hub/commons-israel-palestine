An independent research project by Jasper Blake. It records what MPs said and signed in the Commons. It does not say whether any claim or call is right, and it does not label MPs. Contact: jasparjblake@gmail.com

Contains Parliamentary information licensed under the Open Parliament Licence v3.0. Census and election data from the House of Commons Library, Office for National Statistics and National Records of Scotland, under the Open Government Licence.

Contributions were coded by claude-sonnet-5 (claims prompt v1.1, policy prompt v1.2) and samples were checked by hand.
